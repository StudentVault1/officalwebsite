Privacy Policy

At Student Vault, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, platform, and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and payment details when registering or enrolling in a course.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, device and browser information, and IP address.
  • Course Activity: Progress, quiz scores, and engagement within the course materials.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us to:

  • Deliver both online and offline courses efficiently
  • Improve our content, platform performance, and support
  • Send you important updates, promotional offers, and newsletters (you can opt out anytime)
  • Process payments securely

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share your data with:

  • Trusted service providers (e.g., payment gateways, cloud storage providers)
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures including SSL encryption and secure payment systems to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for promotional communications

6. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance user experience, track analytics, and customize content. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. You’ll be notified via email or website announcements when significant changes are made.
